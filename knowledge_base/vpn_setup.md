# VPN Setup

You need the VPN to reach internal systems when working from home.

1. Install "Pinecrest Connect" from the Software Center.
2. Open the app and sign in with your work email and password.
3. Approve the sign-in on your phone.

If the VPN connects but internal sites do not load, restart the app and check that your Wi-Fi is working.
The VPN disconnects automatically after 12 hours, so sign in again each day.
