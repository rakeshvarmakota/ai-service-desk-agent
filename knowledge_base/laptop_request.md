# Laptop Request and Replacement

New laptops are issued every 4 years, or sooner if the device is broken.

1. Open a ticket with the category "Hardware request".
2. Include your manager's name. Requests need manager approval.
3. Delivery takes 5 to 7 business days after approval.

If your laptop is lost or stolen, report it to the service desk right away so it can be locked remotely.
