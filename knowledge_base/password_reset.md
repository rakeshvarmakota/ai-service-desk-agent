# Password Reset

If you forgot your password or your account is locked:

1. Go to https://reset.pinecrest.example and choose "Forgot password".
2. Enter your work email. A 6-digit code is sent to your phone.
3. Enter the code and choose a new password (at least 12 characters).

Accounts lock after 5 failed sign-in attempts and unlock automatically after 30 minutes.
If you do not receive the code, open a ticket so the service desk can verify your identity.
